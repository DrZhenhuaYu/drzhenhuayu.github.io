<h2 style="font-size:1.35em; margin-top:24px; margin-bottom:10px;">
PhD Opportunities
</h2>


<h3 style="font-size:1.10em; margin-top:15px; margin-bottom:7px;">
Current Opportunities
</h3>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
I welcome enquiries from prospective PhD students interested in robotics, embodied intelligence, wearable sensing, bioelectronics, human–robot interaction, and AI-enabled healthcare systems. Please email me your CV and a brief description of your research interests.
</p>


<h3 style="font-size:1.10em; margin-top:15px; margin-bottom:7px;">
Other PhD Funding Opportunities
</h3>

<ul style="margin-top:6px; margin-bottom:10px; padding-left:24px;">

<li style="margin-bottom:8px;">

<a href="https://www.abdn.ac.uk/study/funding/348">
<b>Aberdeen–CSC PhD Studentship (Chinese students only)</b>
</a>

— The University of Aberdeen may waive international tuition fees, while the China Scholarship Council (CSC) provides a living allowance. The scheme normally involves a two-step application process.

<div style="margin-left:16px; margin-top:6px;">

<b>Step 1:</b> Submit a full PhD application in English to the University of Aberdeen. Applicants normally need to obtain the required University offer before proceeding with the CSC application.

<br><br>

<b>Step 2:</b> Eligible candidates then submit a separate application to the CSC in China according to the CSC application timetable.

</div>

</li>


<li style="margin-bottom:6px;">

<a href="https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-phd-scholarships-for-least-developed-countries-and-vulnerable-states/">
<b>Commonwealth PhD Scholarships</b>
</a>

— Commonwealth PhD Scholarships support eligible applicants from least developed countries and vulnerable states in the Commonwealth to undertake full-time doctoral study at a UK university.

</li>

</ul>

<h2 style="font-size:1.35em; margin-top:24px; margin-bottom:10px;">
Entry Requirements
</h2>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:8px;">
Applicants should normally have, or be about to obtain, a strong undergraduate or Master's degree in an appropriate area of engineering, computer science, robotics, artificial intelligence, or a related discipline.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:8px;">
Applicants whose first language is not English will need to meet the University of Aberdeen's English language requirements and provide appropriate evidence of English proficiency before commencing their studies.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
Please refer to the
<a href="https://www.abdn.ac.uk/study/international/english-requirements/">
University of Aberdeen English Language Requirements
</a>
for further information.
</p>
