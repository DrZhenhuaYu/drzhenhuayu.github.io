<h2 style="font-size:1.35em; margin-top:25px; margin-bottom:12px;">
Bio
</h2>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
Dr Zhenhua Yu is currently an Assistant Professor (Lecturer) at the
<a href="https://www.abdn.ac.uk/ncs/departments/computing-science/" target="_blank">
University of Aberdeen
</a>, UK, and also an Honorary Lecturer in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at
<a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/mechanical-engineering/" target="_blank">
Imperial College London
</a>.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
Before this, he received his PhD degree in Robotics & Artificial Intelligence from the Dyson School of Design Engineering at <b>Imperial College London</b> in 2023, under the supervision of
<a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/p.childs" target="_blank">
Prof. Peter Childs FREng
</a>
and
<a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/t.nanayakkara" target="_blank">
Prof. Thrishantha Nanayakkara
</a>.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
Before joining the University of Aberdeen in November 2024, Dr Zhenhua Yu worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Imperial College London, working with
<a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/r.vaidyanathan" target="_blank">
Prof. Ravi Vaidyanathan
</a>
in the Biomechatronics Lab.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
The aim of our research group is to develop hybrid intelligent robotics and wearable device systems that can interact with the physical world safely and robustly. We hope to prototype novel AI-enabled healthcare robotics systems for research, education, collaboration, and ultimately, commercialisation.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:12px;">
<b>Email:</b> Zhenhua.yu@abdn.ac.uk
</p>

<h2 style="font-size:1.35em; margin-top:28px; margin-bottom:12px;">
Research interests
</h2>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
The rapid growth of ageing populations, together with increasing pressure on healthcare systems and shortages of medical resources, has highlighted the urgent need for advanced bioelectronic devices that support decentralised and personalised healthcare.
</p>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
These technologies include wearable healthcare devices, minimally invasive biosensors, implantable electrodes, closed-loop smart sensing systems, and brain-inspired soft hardware. Our research focuses on developing hybrid intelligent robotics and wearable systems that can safely and robustly interact with humans and the physical world.
</p>

<ul style="margin-top:8px; margin-bottom:10px;">
<li style="margin-bottom:4px;">
Conducting fundamental research to develop soft, wearable, and implantable bioelectronic sensors, e-skins, and soft robotics
</li>

<li style="margin-bottom:4px;">
Developing advanced neuromorphic computation and bioelectronic systems for AI-enabled healthcare robotics
</li>

<li style="margin-bottom:4px;">
Embedded AI, robotic perception, and navigation for human-centric digital healthcare
</li>
</ul>

<p style="margin-top:0; margin-bottom:10px;">
To know more about my research, please visit the
<a href="https://drzhenhuayu.github.io/research/">
Research
</a>
section.
</p>
